## Combined event replay

Each of CONTROL/A6/A7/S1/C1/C2 has its own full position state per instrument/version. A gate applies to raw signals, not to a frozen set of prior trades. Changed occupancy therefore changes later entry decisions. No final journal merge, netting or arithmetic addition substitutes for event replay.

At the next actually observed M15 candle, the pre-existing Stop is checked at Open first and then at the adverse intrabar extreme. A gap uses adverse Open; otherwise Stop fills at its prior active price. On a Stop candle, favorable extreme, activation and trailing are not updated. MAE includes the adverse fill, while MFE excludes the unknown favorable path within the exited candle. Exit timestamps preserve the historical candle Close label and retain actual bar Open/Close endpoints.

For a survived completed candle, extrema accumulate from entry Close plus survived post-entry bars. S1 activates exactly when the favorable extreme reaches at least one fixed initial R. Initial R always equals `abs(entry_price-initial_stop)`. Before activation the initial Stop stays fixed; afterwards the original ATR trail proposal is allowed. Stop is monotone and the new Stop takes effect on the next observed candle. LONG/SHORT are mirrored; signal-candle High/Low never seed activation or extrema.

The original same-Close entry convention and re-entry after a Stop are preserved. State crosses sessions, weekends and years. No forced flat, synthetic candle, exit at the new Stop inside its trigger candle, terminal liquidation or tick-grid adjustment is introduced.
